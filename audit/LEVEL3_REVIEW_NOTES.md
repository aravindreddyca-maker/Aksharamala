Each lesson: one teaching line and five questions with three answers. No pictures, no recorded audio (the Listen button uses the phone's voice).

## English
- L1 Q3: bake / bark / like as rhymes for "bike" (answer "like"). L1 Q5 wording "its own name".
- L3 Q5: "__ip" for a big boat, answer "sh" ("chip" is also a word; the boat clue rules it out).
- L9 Q3: past of "go" tests "went" only. L10 Q2/Q5: "short" and "big" have near-synonym distractors.
- L13: inference answers are the most likely ones, not certain. L14 Q5: the "and" sentence.

## Number Galaxy
- Arithmetic answers are computed in code and re-checked by a test; only the wording needs a look.
- L11, L13, L14 are hand-written: check the clock descriptions (3:15, 7:00, 5:30, quarter to 8, minute hand on 4) and the unit choices.
- Division uses the sign ÷. L5 includes one counting-back pattern (100, 90, 80, 70).

## Telugu
- L3 Q3: is అమ్మ two aksharas (అ, మ్మ)? ఇల్లు and కాకి as two-akshara words.
- L4 Q3/Q4: aksharas of కమలం (క, మ, లం) and పలక. L5 Q1/Q5: the "పండు" clue; a word starting with "పు".
- L6 Q5: is బయట the best opposite of లోపల? L7 spellings: వెళ్తాడు, మొరుగుతుంది, నీళ్లు.
- L8: wrong options are word-order jumbles; confirm the answer is the only natural order.
- L9 Q4: ఉపాధ్యాయుడు. L11/L12 verb forms: కోస్తుంది, నాటారు, పోశారు. L13/L15 teaching lines and stories. L14: ఉంటాము, మంచివాడు.
- L12 Q4 has a deliberately silly distractor (విత్తనం తిన్నారు).

## Hindi
- L2: श्रीमान for "श्र"; पक्का for "क्क". L3: अनार as three akshars. L4: कमल / कलम lookalikes.
- L5: the फूल question and "खुशबूदार". L6: विलोम pairs अच्छा-बुरा, गरम-ठंडा and distractors सुंदर, नरम.
- L8: all five wrong word orders. L9: बाज़ार as स्थान. L10: दौड़ना clue. L11: वाक्य count of 2. L12: "हाथ धोने के बाद".
- L14: gender agreement "मेरा मित्र अच्छा"; पूर्ण विराम (।). L15: आख़िरी (nukta), आरंभ.

## Tamil
- L1: கேள்வி for "கே"; options for "கு" and "கி". L2: தங்கம் (ங் before க); முத்து for "த்து".
- L4: scrambled letters (ப க ல் to பகல், கா க ம் to காகம்); the wrong options are nonsense strings.
- L5: இலை as "பச்சை உறுப்பு"; "கடினமான பொருள்" for கல். L6: சூடு / குளிர், மேலே / கீழே.
- L8: wrong word orders and the spelling மேய்கிறது. L9: is கடை an "இடம்"; "செயல்" distractor.
- L10: படிக்கிறான், ஓடுகிறது, பாடுகிறது. L12: மழைக்குப் பின். L13: "இடம்" and "பாத்திரம்" definitions.
- L14: முற்றுப்புள்ளி and காற்புள்ளி; என் வீடு, என் நண்பன். L15: the காகம் and பானை story.
- Some Tamil prompts in L11, L12, L15 are longer than 110 characters because they carry a short passage.
