All lessons have explicit age-band, difficulty, learning-objective and
prerequisite metadata. English A–Z and the validated Telugu (51), Hindi (46)

age band has different content in every module.

practice only. These four activities are playable but incomplete for mastery
because the pack has no verified stroke geometry or stroke order. They cannot
award verified handwriting mastery. No placeholder pack record was promoted.

## Remaining topic gaps

- Language foundations: recorded/native-verified audio and expanded picture
  vocabulary.
- Ages 5–6: script-combination and phonics progressions, plus verified stroke
  geometry.
- Ages 7–9: word building, sentence reading/writing and broader stories;
  Number Galaxy needs measurement, time and money.
- Ages 10–12: grammar, composition and extended comprehension; Number Galaxy
  needs decimals, geometry, data and advanced word problems.
