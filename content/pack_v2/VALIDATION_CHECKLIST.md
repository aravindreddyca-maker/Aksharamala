# Quality Gates

- [ ] Native educator checks every Telugu, Tamil and Hindi lesson, story and answer.
- [ ] English phonics expert validates phoneme and grapheme sequence.
- [ ] Teacher validates maths progression, distractors and word problems.
- [ ] Every published lesson has complete objectives, prerequisites, examples and age-suitable assessment.
- [ ] Every child-facing `picture_word_match` item has accurate high-resolution image assets.
- [ ] All recorded or TTS audio is heard and signed off on real Android hardware.
- [ ] All questions have exactly one defensible correct answer; avoid leaking answers in prompts.
- [ ] Tracing does not award mastery for arbitrary scribbles.
- [ ] Stories are narrated page by page with matching text and images.
- [ ] Assessment and review measure understanding, not merely repeated tapping.
- [ ] Regional flavors show only their intended language; decide Numbers policy explicitly.
- [ ] Outlined advanced lessons are never accidentally shown as finished content.
- [ ] Accessibility, safe area, landscape responsiveness and offline behaviour tested.
- [ ] Existing profiles/progress migrated safely.
- [ ] Full normal and strict test suites pass without silently skipping known failures.
