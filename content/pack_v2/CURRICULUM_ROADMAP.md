# Curriculum Roadmap (draft for educator review)

| Age band | Language learning | Numbers / mathematics |
|---|---|---|
| 3–4 | Oral language, familiar vocabulary, listen/identify, letter exposure, pictures and rhymes | Count 0–10, one-to-one correspondence, compare groups, sort and basic shapes |
| 5–6 | Letter recognition, phoneme/sound links, guided tracing, combinations, simple word decoding | 0–20, number order, comparison, simple addition/subtraction using objects, patterns |
| 7–9 | Script combinations, reading fluency, spelling, sentences, short stories and literal comprehension | Place value, facts, multiplication/division, measurement, time, money, shapes, graphs, simple fractions |
| 10–12 | Grammar, longer reading, inference, paragraph writing, oral retelling and editing | Multi-step word problems, fractions, decimals, percentages, geometry, data, factors/multiples and ratios |

## Telugu
1. Oral vocabulary and sound awareness; 2. vowels; 3. consonants; 4. letter formation; 5. vowel signs/gunintalu; 6. common conjuncts/ottulu; 7. decoding and vocabulary; 8. sentences; 9. stories; 10. grammar and writing.

## Tamil
1. Oral vocabulary; 2. 12 uyir letters; 3. 18 mei letters; 4. uyirmei combinations; 5. aytham (ஃ); 6. words; 7. sentences; 8. stories; 9. grammar and writing. The 216 uyirmei combinations must be explicitly verified before publication.

## Hindi
1. Oral vocabulary; 2. swar; 3. vyanjan; 4. matras/barakhadi; 5. conjuncts; 6. words; 7. sentences; 8. stories; 9. grammar and writing. Handle regional differences and spelling conventions with educator review.

## English
1. Oral phonemic awareness; 2. letters and sounds; 3. blending/segmenting CVC words; 4. digraphs and long-vowel patterns; 5. sight words and decoding; 6. sentences; 7. stories; 8. comprehension, grammar and composition.

## Numbers
1. Number sense 0–10; 2. count and compare 0–20; 3. add/subtract with manipulatives; 4. place value and number patterns; 5. multiplication/division; 6. measurement, time and money; 7. fractions/decimals; 8. geometry/data; 9. percentages/ratios and multi-step problems.

## Mastery rule suggestion
Three varied practice items followed by a separate assessment, with ≥80% accuracy across at least two sessions, no repeated identical answer positions, and review after 1, 3 and 7 days. These are proposed product rules, not research-validated thresholds.
