Some letters have **no sensible word that starts with them** in Telugu, Tamil and Hindi (for example ఙ, ఞ, ణ, ங், ழ், ङ, ञ). Indian primers teach these inside a word. They are marked "letter only" instead of inventing a forced word.

**Pictures needed: 129 in total** (English 26, Telugu 41, Tamil 22, Hindi 40). None exist in the app today.

## What I would do once you approve

1. Write the new words into a **separate supplement file** (the pack stays untouched), in the same record format, so the importer checks them like everything else.
2. Add them to the single app's letter lessons: *see the letter → hear it → pick the picture → "A for Apple"*.
3. List the 129 pictures in one sheet (word, language, simple description) so they can be generated and dropped in. Until a picture exists, that letter is shown as "needs image" and stays out of the child's path.
4. A Telugu, Tamil and Hindi educator signs off the new words and the phone voice before children see them.
